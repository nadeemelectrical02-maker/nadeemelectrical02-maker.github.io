Pehle ek sachchi baat

Ye mini app khud paisay nahi deti — na koi app sirf banane se paisay deta hai. Paisa logon se ata hai: jo log aap ki kahaniyan parhte hain, videos dekhte hain, un ke samne ane wale ishtehar (ads) aur un ki pasand hi kamai banti hai. Is liye asal formula ye hai:

Roz ka acha content → Zyada log → Har platform par ads → Kamai

Ye app is safar ka control room hai: yahan se aap ko pata chalta hai paisa kahan kahan se ayega, kis cheez ki kitni shartein hain, aur aaj aap kahan khare hain.

Kamai ke 5 Raste

Har raste ki shartein aur aap ki mojooda halat — koi jhooti umeed nahi, jo hai wahi likha hai.

1

Blog — Google AdSense Sab se qareeb

Aap ka blog sukoonbayaan.blogspot.com AdSense se jud chuka hai. Log kahani parhne ayenge, Google un ko ishtehar dikhayega, aur har ishtehar par aap ko hissa milega. Pinterest aur WhatsApp se ane wala har reader seedha is kamai ko barhata hai.

Halat: AdSense review chal raha hai (“Getting ready”) — manzoori mein din ya haftay lag sakte hain.
Baqi qadam: Blog posts 10 se barha kar 15–20 karna, aur AdSense mein Payments ki maloomat (“Tell us about you”) mukammal karna — ye sirf aap khud kar sakte hain.
2

YouTube — Partner Program

Shorts rozana lagane se channel barhta hai. Partner Program mein shamil hote hi lambi videos aur Shorts dono par ishtehar aana shuru ho jate hain. Aap ki nature wali lambi video is safar ki pehli seedhi hai.

Shartein: 1,000 subscribers aur ya to pichle 12 mah mein 4,000 ghante watch time, ya pichle 90 din mein 1 crore (10 million) Shorts views.
Halat: Islamic Prayer Hub channel par 987 subscribers the (aakhri check par) — sirf 13 kam. Us account ki recovery hi is raste ki chabi hai.
3

Facebook — Video Monetization

Wohi Shorts jo YouTube par lagti hain, Facebook Page par bhi lagti hain. Page ke followers aur video views barhne par Facebook bhi videos mein ishtehar chalata hai aur kamai deta hai.

Qadam: Personal profile ke bajaye proper Facebook Page “Sukoon Bayan” banana, phir roz wohi 2 Shorts wahan lagana. Ek hi mehnat, do jagah kamai.
4

CapCut Templates — Creator Rewards

Ye sab se mukhtalif rasta hai: aap ek khoobsurat template banate hain (Quran ayat, dua, quote wali design), doosre creators us template ko apni videos mein istemal karte hain — aur har istemal par CapCut aap ko inaam (reward) deta hai. Ek acha template hazaron dafa istemal ho sakta hai.

Halat: CapCut Pro aap ke paas active hai aur 25 templates ka plan tayyar hai. Template publish sirf mobile app se hota hai.
5

Pinterest + WhatsApp / Telegram — Muft Traffic

Ye do raste khud paisa nahi dete, magar kamai wale raston ko log pahunchate hain. Pinterest pin par click → banda YouTube video ya blog par. WhatsApp Channel aur Telegram Channel par roz ki post ka link → purane readers roz wapas. Traffic zyada, to AdSense aur YouTube ki kamai zyada — simple hisaab hai.

Halat: Pinterest jud chuka hai aur nature video ki pin “Sukoon Videos” board mein live hai. WhatsApp Channel banana aap ke phone se hoga — link mile to blog par button lag jayega.
